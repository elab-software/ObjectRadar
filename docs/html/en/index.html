THE PROBLEM

You know it's nearby.
But where is it?

You can know that a Bluetooth device is nearby without having a practical way to locate it precisely. Object Radar explores the Bluetooth information available to iOS to turn that signal into useful proximity guidance.

LIMITATIONS

Bluetooth has physical limits.

Bluetooth signal strength can help estimate proximity, but walls, furniture, orientation and other environmental factors can affect the result.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What can the application detect?

ObjectRadar scans nearby Bluetooth Low Energy advertisements and analyzes the information made available by the devices. Detection depends on the device, its advertising behavior and the information exposed by its Bluetooth protocol.

How does proximity guidance work?

The application observes changes in Bluetooth signal strength while you move. These changes can indicate whether you are getting closer to or farther from a device, but they cannot provide a precise distance.

Why can some Bluetooth devices remain invisible?

A device may not advertise continuously, may use a protocol that does not expose enough information, or may be affected by Bluetooth and environmental limitations.

What are the limitations of Bluetooth tracking?

Bluetooth signal strength is affected by distance, obstacles, reflections, device orientation and the surrounding environment. Proximity guidance should therefore be understood as an indication rather than an exact measurement.
